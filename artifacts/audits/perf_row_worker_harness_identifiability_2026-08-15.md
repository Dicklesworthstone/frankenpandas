## Why this audit exists

Two fleet-wide findings, neither measured by me:

* **Worker disagreement.** frankenscipy ran the SAME cubic `splu` cell on two rch
  workers and got 1.2693x and 0.0093x — a 13.6x swing — with BOTH A/A nulls
  PASSING. An A/A null controls WITHIN-invocation noise; it is blind to
  between-worker CPU model, cache, bandwidth and contention.
* **Harness disagreement is the same size.** frankenlibc measured malloc/free on
  ONE worker (hz2) with two separately-sanctioned harnesses and got 5.9459x and
  12.385414x — a ~2x spread — again with both nulls in tolerance. A passing null
  does not certify that a harness measures what you think it measures.

So a row must name BOTH the worker it ran on and the harness that timed it.
frankenfs made this a gate and retro-flagged 166 rows; franken_numpy replicated a
win on a second worker and RETRACTED a sub-claim doing so.
